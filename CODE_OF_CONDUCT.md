# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors, regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers directly.
